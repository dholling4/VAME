Figure 1 shows how the dance naturally breaks into a few repeating movement “zones.” Panel A is a map of where the dancer spends most time in movement space. Panel B shows how often the dance switches from one short movement pattern to another. Panel C traces sample motion paths, with orange points marking moments of strong turning/spinning. In plain terms, the model finds that the dance is not random: it cycles through stable movement types, and turns/spins form a distinct, recurring part of that cycle.
